What is Text to HTML Ratio

The text to HTML ratio is a metric that compares the amount of visible text on a webpage to the amount of HTML code used to create that page. It's expressed as a percentage, calculated by dividing the number of text characters by the total number of characters in the HTML source code. This ratio helps assess the balance between content and code on a webpage.

What does text to HTML ratio mean in SEO

In SEO (Search Engine Optimization), the text to HTML ratio is considered a potential factor in how search engines evaluate a webpage's content quality and relevance. A higher ratio generally indicates that a page has more substantial content relative to its code, which can be seen as a positive signal. However, it's important to note that this ratio is just one of many factors in SEO, and its direct impact on rankings is debated among experts.

Best text to HTML ratio for SEO

There's no universally agreed-upon "best" text to HTML ratio for SEO, as search engines use complex algorithms with numerous factors. However, many SEO professionals suggest aiming for a ratio between 25% and 70%. It's more important to ensure your content is valuable to users and properly structured with appropriate HTML elements.
