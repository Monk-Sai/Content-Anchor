N-grams SEO

N-grams in SEO are sequences of consecutive items like words, characters, etc. from a website or webpage. They are used in natural language processing to analyze text patterns and relationships.

What are Ngrams

Ngrams are sequences or combinations of words used in natural language processing. They help computers understand the patterns and relationships between words in a webpage or website. Essentially, they are group of consecutive words that are present frequently around the content. For instance, “Ngrams SEO” is a bigram (a sequence of two words). By analyzing these word combinations, computers can better understand and process human language. N-grams are fundamental building blocks for various language-related applications including search engines.

Frequently Asked Questions


What are different types of N-grams?

N-grams are basically single or combination of multiple words together and their frequency through out the webpage. Some of the types of N-grams are, Bi-grams(two words) and Tri-grams(three words).
